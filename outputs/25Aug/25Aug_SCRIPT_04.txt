TITLE: The Escalating Crisis of Global Heat
------------------------------
Europe is grappling with a grim milestone as back-to-back heatwaves have been linked to at least 35,000 excess deaths. This surge in mortality underscores the intensifying health risks posed by a warming climate, as public health systems struggle to manage the lethal consequences of prolonged, extreme temperatures. The crisis is not confined to land, as the world's oceans reached their highest recorded temperatures this August. This thermal anomaly serves as a stark indicator of accelerating climate change, with scientists warning that the warming of the seas threatens marine ecosystems and disrupts global weather patterns. Meanwhile, in Pakistan, the reality of these shifting conditions is a daily struggle for survival. Residents are increasingly forced to improvise, relying on basic tools like blocks of ice to endure persistent, life-threatening heat that continues to push the limits of human endurance.
