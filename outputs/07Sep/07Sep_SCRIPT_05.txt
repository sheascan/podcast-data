By becoming the first Italian to win at Monza since 1966, Antonelli has not only extended his lead in the drivers' standings but has also cemented his status as a national icon. The implications for the championship are profound; he has effectively neutralized the threat from his closest rivals, proving that he can extract maximum performance from his machinery regardless of his starting position. The championship battle is far from over, but the momentum is undeniably with the young Italian, who seems to thrive under the weight of expectation.
