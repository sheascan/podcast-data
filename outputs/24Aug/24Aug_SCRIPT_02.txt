In a direct act of retaliation, Canada has fired back with its own set of tariffs against the United States.

If they lower rates to stimulate growth, they risk letting inflation spiral out of control. It is a precarious balancing act where there may be no graceful landing.

As we track the developments in this trade war, one thing is certain: the repercussions will be felt for years to come. Whether this leads to a new negotiation table or a deeper divide is the question on every analyst’s mind. But for tonight, the markets have spoken, and they are terrified.
