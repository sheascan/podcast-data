The legal dispute, which had cast a long shadow over the integrity and governance of African football for months, concluded with the panel determining that the initial disciplinary measures lacked proper procedural standing and evidential merit. For the Senegalese football federation and millions of supporters in Dakar and beyond, the decision marks the ultimate vindication of their on-field triumphs.
